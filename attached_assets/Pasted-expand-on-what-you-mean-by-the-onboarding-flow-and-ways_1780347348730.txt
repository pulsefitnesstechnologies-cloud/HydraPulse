I reviewed the current onboarding.tsx file in your repo. Here's a detailed breakdown.
Current Onboarding Flow
Your onboarding is a multi-step carousel with 4 screens:

Welcome to HydraPulse – High-level intro
How PPG Works – Explains the science (Photoplethysmography)
Phone-Only Mode – How to use the camera torch scan
Privacy First – Reassures users about data handling

Strengths:

Good visual design with icons and colors.
Covers the unique value proposition (PPG + science).
Privacy emphasis is smart for a health app.

Main Problems:

Too long and explanatory — 4 educational slides is a lot for new users. Most people want to try the app quickly, not read about the technology.
Heavy on "how it works" instead of "what's in it for me".
Risk of high drop-off — users who get bored or overwhelmed will never reach the main app.


How to Improve the Onboarding Flow
Here’s a practical redesign plan:
Recommended New Structure (3 Steps Max)





























StepTitleGoalKey Content1WelcomeHook the user emotionallyBig headline + benefit-focused subtitle + "Let's Get Started"2Quick StartGet them scanning fastSimple 3-step visual guide ("1. Place finger → 2. Hold still → 3. Get score") + Demo animation3Permissions & PrivacyBuild trustCamera + HealthKit permissions + short privacy note
Key Improvements:

Make it Benefit-First, Not Tech-First
Instead of starting with "Welcome to HydraPulse", start with:"See how hydrated you really are — in 12 seconds."

Reduce Cognitive Load
Cut technical explanations (PPG science) to a small optional "Learn More" link.
Focus on outcomes: better energy, better recovery, better workouts.

Add Interactivity
Include a short interactive demo (e.g. fake scan animation).
Allow skipping after the first screen.

Strong Call-to-Action
End with a prominent "Start My First Scan" button that feels exciting.

Personalization
Ask for daily goal (e.g. 2.5L, 3L, etc.) during onboarding.
