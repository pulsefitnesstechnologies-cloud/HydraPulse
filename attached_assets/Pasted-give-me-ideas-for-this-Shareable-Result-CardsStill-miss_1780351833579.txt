1. Shareable Result Cards (1–2 days effort)
This is one of the best organic marketing features you can add right now.
Core Idea:
After a scan completes (on the Results screen), show a beautiful, ready-to-share card that users can screenshot or save directly.
Design Ideas (Ranked)
Best Option: “Science-Backed Hydration Snapshot”

Large central score (e.g. “4.2” with “Great” label)
“Live Torch Reading” or “Watch Baseline” tag
Key metrics: HR, HRV, Signal Quality, Estimated Hydration
Timestamp + date
Subtle watermark: “HydraPulse • Measured with PPG”
Background: Dark with subtle water ripple or gradient
Brand colors (teal/blue)

Alternative Versions:

“Streak Edition” — if they hit a streak milestone
“Weekly Summary” card
“Before vs After” (if they do multiple scans)

Features to Include:

“Save to Photos” button (using expo-media-library)
“Share” button (using expo-sharing)
Copy text version for social media
Option to toggle “Include Health Data” (for privacy-conscious users)

Implementation Tip:
Create a new component ShareableResultCard.tsx that takes the scan result as props and renders a clean card. Then add it prominently on the Results screen with confetti on good scores.

2. Legal / Privacy Policy (Required for App Store)
You must have these before submitting to Apple or Google.
What You Need:

Privacy Policy (Most Important)
Terms of Service (Recommended)
Data Handling Notice in the app (Settings → Privacy)

Recommended Approach:
A. Quick & Professional Route (Recommended)

Use a service like Termly.io, Iubenda, or FreePrivacyPolicy.com (paid versions are better)
Generate a policy tailored to:
Camera access
HealthKit / biometric data
PPG measurements
Local storage (AsyncStorage)

Cost: $10–50 one-time or yearly

B. DIY Version
I can help you write a solid basic Privacy Policy. Key sections to include:

What data we collect (scan results, HealthKit read-only data)
What we don’t do (no selling data, no cloud upload unless user chooses)
Camera usage (only used during active scan, not background)
Data retention (local only)
User rights (delete all data)
